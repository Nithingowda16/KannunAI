EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of January 15, 2026, by and between Lexora Tech Corp ("Company") and Jane Doe ("Employee").

SECTION 1. POSITION AND DUTIES
1.1 Position. Employee shall serve as Senior Software Engineer.
1.2 Duties. Employee shall perform all duties customary to the position.

SECTION 2. COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
2.1 Base Salary. The Company shall pay Employee a base salary of $160,000 per annum, payable in accordance with regular payroll.
2.2 Bonuses. Discretionary performance bonus up to 15% annually.

SECTION 3. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD
3.1 Termination by Notice. Either party may terminate this employment upon 30 days written notice.
3.2 Severance. In the event of termination without cause, Employee shall receive 2 months base salary.

SECTION 4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CONFIDENTIALITY
4.1 Inventions. All work product, inventions, and patent rights created during employment belong exclusively to Company.

SECTION 5. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
5.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
